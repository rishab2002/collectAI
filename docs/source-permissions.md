# Asking a manufacturer for permission

The catalogs and photos on Mini GT/TrueScale, Para64 (Paragon), Inno64 and
Schuco's websites can't be imported without the maker's permission — see
README §11 for what each site's terms say. Many makers are glad to have their
range in a collector catalog that credits them and links back, so asking is
worth it. Below is a request you can adapt.

## Where to send it

| Brand | Contact |
|---|---|
| Mini GT / TrueScale Miniatures | info@tsm-model.com (from tsm-models.com's terms page) |
| Para64 / Paragon Models | the contact page on paragonmodelstore.com |
| Inno64 / INNO Models | the contact page on inno-models.com |
| Schuco | the contact form on schuco.de (Minimax Import & Export Co. Ltd., Macau) |

## The request

> **Subject:** Permission to list [Brand] models in a collector catalog
>
> Hello,
>
> I run collectAI (collectai.club), a free catalog where diecast collectors
> browse models by brand, line and series. I'd like to include the [Brand]
> range, and I'm asking before I do.
>
> Specifically, I'd like permission to use:
>
> 1. **Product facts** from your website: model name, item number, scale,
>    colour/livery and release date.
> 2. **One product photo per model**, shown on that model's page with the
>    credit "Photo © [Brand]" and a link to the product on your site.
>
> I wouldn't use prices or descriptions, and I'd remove anything you ask me to.
> If you have a press kit, product feed or preferred image set, I'd gladly use
> that instead.
>
> Could you reply to confirm whether this is OK, and how you'd like [Brand]
> credited?
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name]

## If they say yes

1. Save the reply (PDF or screenshot) somewhere permanent — it is the licence.
2. Note the brand, the date and exactly what was allowed in CLAUDE.md under
   *Data sources*.
3. Then an importer for that source can be built, crediting the photos the way
   they asked (the `photos.credit` and `photos.license` columns exist for this).
